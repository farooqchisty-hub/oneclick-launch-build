Monthly allowance and buffer

Monthly customers get a fixed number of new accepted listings each month. New directories the finder adds beyond that wait in the customer's own buffer and are used in later months, for as long as Monthly stays on. The same page covers a finder second look that qualifies more of the sites it rejects, within the existing rules.

So today a big finder week (the 19 Nov import added 918 directories) goes to every Monthly customer at once, and a quiet week gives them almost nothing.

The change

Finder adds directories→a pilot gets accepted there (payable)→each Monthly customer's buffer→up to the monthly allowance this month→the rest waits for next month
  • One number: the monthly allowance (MONTHLY.allowance), counted in accepted listings, not sites. The engine works through as many buffered directories as it takes to reach it.
  • The minimum guarantee stays (25 a month on top of a launch). The allowance is the most a month gives; the guarantee is the least.
  • The buffer is per customer and keeps growing while Monthly is on: every directory that became payable after their launch and that they have not been submitted to yet. Unused allowance does not roll over; unused directories do.
  • Best directories first: proven before badge, then the directory's acceptance rate across products, then authority, then the oldest wait.
  • Cancel: the buffer stops with the paid period. Come back: it resumes where it stopped (it is computed, nothing is lost).
  • What counts: a listing accepted on a new directory. Resubmits after a rejection and keep-alive resubmits are upkeep of the listings the customer already has, so they stay outside the allowance and keep running.

Why sites and listings differ

On paid-plan directories the five test products got 3,429 accepted of 6,694 tried (51%). So 400 new sites in a month is about 200 accepted listings for one customer, not 400. Supply per Monthly customer, measured:
