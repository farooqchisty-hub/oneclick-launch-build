Summary

Today the app sends 15 kinds of customer email and 2 owner alert paths, all transactional, all plain text, all through one Cloudflare Email Sending binding. There is no marketing or lifecycle email, no unsubscribe, no consent record and no email capture on the free preview. The core is solid. The gaps are money mails (failed payments, cancels, manual refunds), the mails that would get customers more listings (badge block, company email), and everything after the final report.

The 5 that matter most

  1. T2 Launch started. The first mail after paying. It sets the promise, the exact 48-hour deadline and the automatic refund, and gets the first dashboard visit.
  2. T4 and T5, the 48-hour result. The guarantee moment. Met: proof. Missed: an honest refund that keeps trust. T4 can be lost today when the quota defers it.
  3. N2 and N3, badge block and company email. The only two things that add listings and need the customer. Badge listings count toward the 48-hour number once we see the badge.
  4. M5 Everything free month. Already paid for, not yet started. Starting it right after the report is the easiest Monthly subscriber we will ever get.
  5. M4 Monthly for Launch buyers. Launch stops at 300, so hundreds of catalog directories that fit are still untouched. Monthly works through them. Strongest new revenue line.

Most urgent gap outside the top 5: T13 Monthly payment failed. Today a failed renewal pauses Monthly silently.

What blocks marketing mail

  1. No postal address. LEGAL_NAME and LEGAL_ADDRESS are unset and the seller details in site-config.js are empty. CAN-SPAM needs a postal address in every commercial mail, and the privacy page cannot name the controller. Every group C marketing mail waits on this.
  2. No unsubscribe or suppression anywhere in the code: no link, no header, no table.
  3. No consent capture. Checkout offers no opt-out for product news, the preview takes no email, and the checkout email is optional.
  4. No room in the quota. About 200 a day for the whole Cloudflare account, shared with Closefast and with owner alerts, neither counted. Marketing needs its own provider and subdomain.
  5. Sender mismatch. From is the brand, not Farooq, on mail.thereisnomoat.fyi, with links to oneclick-launch.pages.dev.
What can ship now, with no legal blocker

All of group A and group B, plus the two service mails in group C (M3 founder check-in and M6 milestones), because none of them promote anything. That is the build order's first two phases. Marketing starts once the seller's name and address exist and the unsubscribe is built.

How mail is sent today

  • Cloudflare Email Sending, send_email binding EMAIL, allowed sender hello@mail.thereisnomoat.fyi.
  • From name is always the brand ("One-Click Launch", from BRAND_NAME). The name part of MAIL_FROM is ignored.
  • Reply-to support@thereisnomoat.fyi, forwarded to Farooq's Gmail and filed in the admin inbox.
  • APP_ORIGIN is unset, so every link points at https://oneclick-launch.pages.dev.
  • Footer: brand and support address. LEGAL_NAME and LEGAL_ADDRESS print only when set, and they are not set.
  • Plain text plus a minimal HTML copy (textHtml). No images, no open or click tracking.

Sending limits in mail.ts

  • About 200 a day for the whole Cloudflare account.
  • RESERVE_AFTER = 120 (lowered from 150 on 19 November because Closefast sends from the same account and is not counted). After 120 of our own sends in a UTC day, only priority kinds go.
  • Priority kinds: signin, started, delivered, refund, owner_alert.
  • The count skips owner alerts. They still use the same Cloudflare limit, up to 6 an hour.
  • A deferred mail claims nothing, so a later call can send it. A transient failure releases its key for the next tick.

Gaps found in the code

  1. Two mails are lost when the reserve defers them. The 48-hour "met" result is sent once, after the order is marked checked, and the Monthly report is sent once from the renewal webhook. Nothing sends either one again. Every other deferrable kind is retried by its cron.
  2. The real headroom is smaller than 120. Closefast and owner alerts share the roughly 200 and are not counted. On a busy day Cloudflare can refuse even priority mail with E_DAILY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED, and the sign-in endpoint then answers "couldn't send the sign-in link".
  3. No mail at all for: a failed Monthly renewal (status on_hold), Monthly cancelled, Monthly ended, refunds made by an admin or in the Dodo dashboard (only the automatic 48-hour refund mails the customer), the add-on bought later from the dashboard, and a short Monthly month (the free next month is only an admin alert).
  4. Nothing asks for the badge block or the company email by mail, though both add listings and only the customer can do them.
  5. A directory that needs the customer waits for the next daily digest, up to a day and more.
  6. The daily digest never stops. It goes every day directories write, for delivered orders too.
  7. The Google sign-in mail repeats daily with no end, and the add-on has no reminder after 24 hours. Nothing decides what happens to the $39 if Google is never connected.
  8. Sender and links don't match the brand: From says "One-Click Launch", not Farooq, on the directory-inbox domain, with links on pages.dev.
  9. Subject typo: "Your One-Click Launch launch report is ready".
  10. No marketing foundation: no unsubscribe, consent record or suppression list. The preview takes no email. The checkout email is optional, so many pending orders have none.
  11. No first name, country or time zone is stored (Dodo has the name; Cloudflare gives country and time zone on every request). Mails can't greet by name or arrive at a local hour.
  12. Dashboard purchases carry no UTM: attachCheckout (Monthly and the add-on) stores no attribution, so no mail can be credited with a Monthly start.

Voice and format

Voice

  • From "Farooq from One-Click Launch" (the brand part still comes from BRAND_NAME). Replies go to a mailbox Farooq reads.
  • Open with Hi {first_name}, or just Hi, when there is no name. Never "Dear", never "Hey there".
  • Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Plain words. Say the number first: the count, the promise, the deadline.
  • One link that matters per mail. Dashboard links open the exact tab (listings, inbox, badge, google, company, report).
  • No exclamation marks as a rule. No em dashes or en dashes; ranges read "5 to 10".
  • Sign off with "Farooq". No title, logo or signature block.
  • Honest about misses. Never invent urgency or deadlines that don't exist.

Format

  • Plain text first. The HTML copy is the same text with links, as textHtml does today. No images, no buttons.
  • Dates like humanTime: "Tuesday 24 November 2026 at 14:05 UTC". Money in USD: "$29".
  • Subjects under about 55 characters, product name in transactional subjects so they thread per product.
  • Preview text adds to the subject instead of repeating it. Put it first in the HTML as a hidden span.
  • Footer, added by sendOnce: brand, support address, legal name and postal address. Marketing adds one line: why they get it and a one-click stop link.
  • Tokens look like {product}. [Conditions look like this] and are not part of the text.
Footer on every marketing mail
-- One-Click Launch, {legal_name}, {postal_address} You're getting this because you bought {plan} for {product}. It's a product note, not a receipt. Stop these: {unsubscribe_link}

The first-name token needs a small change: store the customer name Dodo returns with the payment (fulfill in orders.ts), first word only, and fall back to no name when it looks like a company or is empty.

  • Primary purpose rule (CAN-SPAM). A mail that mixes a receipt and a pitch is judged by its subject and by what comes first. Keep T and N mails free of pitches. The Everything free-month paragraph in T6 is part of what they bought, so it is stated as fact and placed last. No Monthly offer in Launch buyers' transactional mail.
  • CAN-SPAM (US) for marketing. Honest From and subject, the sender's postal address, an opt-out that works for at least 30 days after each send and is honored within 10 business days (we do it instantly), and the mail recognisable as promotional unless they opted in. The footer line "It's a product note, not a receipt" does that without breaking the voice.
  • GDPR and ePrivacy (EU), UK GDPR and PECR (UK). Marketing email to individuals needs consent, except the soft opt-in for customers: the address came from a sale, the mail is about similar products of ours, and they were given a simple way to refuse when we collected it and in every mail since. Monthly, the add-on and a second launch are similar products, so customers qualify once checkout offers the opt-out. People who never bought (preview, abandoned checkout) need consent. The UK regulator reads "negotiations for a sale" to include a started purchase; EU countries read it more narrowly, so EU abandoned checkouts get M2a only.
  • CASL (Canada). Implied consent lasts 2 years after a purchase and 6 months after an inquiry. Same identification, address and unsubscribe rules.
  • Record the basis on every contact: consent, soft opt-in, or none, with the time, the source (checkout, preview, dashboard) and the country. Country and time zone come free from Cloudflare on the request (request.cf). When the country is unknown, apply the strictest rule.
  • Privacy page. It already lists "Contact you about the service after your order ends" under legitimate interests with a right to object. It still needs the controller's name and address, a consent line for preview emails, and the marketing provider in the processor list.

Capture changes

  • Checkout: under the email field, one line and an unticked box. "I'll email you about your launch. Now and then I'll send a note about new directories or Monthly. Tick to skip those." That is the soft opt-in's "chance to refuse".
  • Preview: "Email me this preview" plus an unticked "and send me 2 short follow-ups". Only the box allows M1b and M1c.
  • Dashboard: a preferences panel: product news on or off, weekly went-live note on or off, launch updates daily or "only when you need me".

Unsubscribe mechanics

  • A signed per-person token link, one click, no login, working for at least 60 days after each send.
  • List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click headers on every marketing mail. If the Cloudflare binding can't set custom headers, that is one more reason marketing goes through its own provider.
  • Unsubscribing stops group C (marketing and service) at once. T and N keep going, because they are the service.
  • Bounces and complaints from either provider land in the same suppression table.
The postal address blocks all marketing

4. Sending infrastructure

Today

  • One Cloudflare Email Sending account, about 200 a day, shared with Closefast.
  • OCL holds deferrable mail after 120 of its own sends. Closefast and owner alerts (up to 6 an hour) aren't counted, so the real headroom on a busy day is unknown.
  • When the account limit is hit, even sign-in links fail, and the sign-in page says "couldn't send the sign-in link".
  • Customer mail goes from the same registrable domain that signs up to 700+ directories (thereisnomoat.fyi). That domain's reputation should never carry customer mail.

Recommended

  • Transactional and notifications stay on Cloudflare Email Sending, moved to mail.oneclicklaunch.dev, From "Farooq from One-Click Launch", reply-to farooq@oneclicklaunch.dev routed to the support inbox. Set APP_ORIGIN so links are on the same brand domain.
  • Marketing on its own provider and subdomain, news.oneclicklaunch.dev, so it can never eat the transactional quota and its complaints never touch receipts or sign-in. The Worker still decides and logs every send; the provider only delivers and reports bounces and complaints back.
  • thereisnomoat.fyi stays the directory inbox only.

Quota fixes, before any volume grows

  1. Count every send on the account: owner alerts, and Closefast's sends (or move Closefast to its own Cloudflare account). Ask Cloudflare to raise the daily limit once volume justifies it.
  2. Add to the priority kinds: result (T4), trial_end (T11), Monthly started (T10), payment failed (T13), cancelled (T14), other refunds (T7).
  3. Retry T4 and T12 from a cron when deferred, like retryStarted, so nothing is lost.
  4. Tier the reserve: under 100 sends everything goes; 100 to 140 only transactional plus N1 and N5; over 140 only priority kinds.
  5. Replace the forever-daily digest with T3 in launch week and N6 weekly after delivery. That alone takes most of the daily load off.

Marketing provider: what to pick

The program is plain text composed in the Worker, at small volume, so it needs an API more than an editor. Recommended: Amazon SES on news.oneclicklaunch.dev. It costs $0.10 per 1,000 mails, has its own reputation, and reports bounces and complaints by webhook; we build the unsubscribe page, which we need for preferences anyway. SES has to approve production access before it sends to anyone. Any other provider works if it has: an API, a dedicated authenticated subdomain, one-click unsubscribe headers, and bounce and complaint webhooks.

5. Measurement

Links and clicks

  • UTMs on every link: utm_source=email, utm_medium = transactional, notification, service or marketing, utm_campaign = the mail id (t02, m04a), utm_content = link or variant.
  • The dashboard is hash-routed, so the query goes before the hash: https://oneclicklaunch.dev/app/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=marketing&utm_campaign=m04a#/order/{order}.
  • Clicks through our own redirect, /r/{mail_key}/{n}, logged first-party. Never on sign-in links.
  • Opens are not a metric. Apple Mail preloads images, so opens lie. Use clicks, replies and conversions.
  • Credit dashboard purchases. attachCheckout stores no attribution today; pass the UTMs from the dashboard URL into the Monthly and add-on checkouts. The order columns already exist.

Conversion events (server side)

  • monthly_started, monthly_trial_started, monthly_kept (past the first charge)
  • addon_bought, second_product_bought, referral_purchase
  • checkout_recovered (a pending order paid after M2)
  • badge_placed (badge_ok flips), own_email_verified, google_connected, directory_reply_answered
  • testimonial_received (admin tag), unsubscribed, complaint

Each joins to mail_sent by customer and time: 7 days after a click for marketing, last touch.

Holdouts and health

  • 10% holdout on M2, M4, M5, M9 and M11: a fixed hash of customer and flow. The held-back mail is written to mail_sent with status holdout, so both groups are the same kind of people.
  • Readouts: M4 and M5, Monthly start rate 30 days after delivery. M2, paid within 7 days. M9 and M11, Monthly restarts within 60 days. Read only after about 100 people in each group; before that, treat results as direction, not proof.
  • No holdout on transactional, notifications or service mails: they are the service. For N2 and N3, compare badge and company email rates before and after.
  • Weekly health per class: hard bounces under 2%, complaints under 0.1%, marketing unsubscribes under 0.5% a send, founder mail replies over 2%. Add a mail view to the admin funnel (/api/admin/funnel).
